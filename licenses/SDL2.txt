Note (ToroidAMP-specific, RC-069-003B): pygame-ce also bundles several
SDL2 companion/helper libraries used for image, font, and mixer
support (SDL2_image, SDL2_mixer, SDL2_ttf) and their own transitive
codec dependencies (libjpeg, libpng, libtiff, libwebp, libogg, libopus,
libwavpack, FreeType). These are all established, permissively-licensed
open-source multimedia libraries (zlib/libpng-style, BSD-style, or
FreeType's own FTL/BSD-style license, depending on the specific
component) — their exact individual license texts were NOT
independently re-verified file-by-file in this cut; see the
NEEDS VERIFICATION entries in
docs/release/RC_069_003B_license_and_docs.md's legal-readiness matrix.
None of them are believed to carry copyleft/LGPL-or-stronger terms, but
that belief is not yet independently confirmed to the same standard as
the components with a dedicated license file in this directory.
